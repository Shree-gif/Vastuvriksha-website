# Logo Setup Instructions

## Steps to Add Your Vastuvriksha Logo

Your logo has been integrated into the website code! Now you just need to save your logo images to the correct location.

### Step 1: Save Your Logo Files

1. Save the **first logo image** (the one with beige background) as `logo-small.png` 
2. Save the **second logo image** (the one with white background) as `logo-large.png`
3. Place both files in the `public` folder of your project

**File Locations:**
- `E:\test git\Vastuvriksha-website\public\logo-small.png` (for navbar)
- `E:\test git\Vastuvriksha-website\public\logo-large.png` (for hero section)

### Step 2: Recommended Image Specifications

**For logo-small.png (Navbar):**
- Height: ~200-300 pixels
- Transparent background (PNG format)
- Should show clearly against white navbar background

**For logo-large.png (Hero Section):**
- Height: ~300-400 pixels
- Transparent or white background (PNG format)
- Should show clearly against dark hero background

### Step 3: Refresh Your Browser

Once you've placed the logo files in the `public` folder, refresh your browser to see your beautiful Vastuvriksha logo in:
1. **Navbar** - Small logo next to "Vastuvriksha" text (top left)
2. **Homepage Hero** - Larger logo with floating animation next to the tagline

### Features Added:
- ✨ Smooth hover effect on navbar logo
- 🎭 Floating animation on hero logo
- 📱 Responsive sizing for all devices
- 🎨 Professional drop shadow effects

---

**Need Help?** If the logos don't appear after placing the files, try:
1. Clear your browser cache
2. Hard refresh (Ctrl + F5 on Windows)
3. Restart the development server (`npm run dev`)
